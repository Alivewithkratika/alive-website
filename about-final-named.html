WHY ALIVE EXISTS

Somewhere along the way, we can lose touch with ourselves.

You are not broken.

Somewhere between growing up and becoming who we were expected to be, many of us stop asking what actually makes us feel alive.

We become responsible. We achieve. We keep going. And somewhere along the way, we can lose touch with ourselves.

I’m Kratika.

I created ALIVE because I believe life is meant to be more than simply getting through it.

My work is about helping people pause, understand themselves, reconnect with their energy, explore what genuinely matters to them, and create a life that feels more like their own.

Not a perfect life.
Not someone else’s version of success.
A life that leaves you feeling fulfilled.

From surviving to living.
