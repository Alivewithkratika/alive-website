MEET KRATIKA

The person behind ALIVE.

MY STORY

I didn’t just find a better life.
I found myself.

At some point, life became less about what I want and more about what I should do.

Study. Work. Achieve. Be responsible. Make everyone proud. Keep going.

And you did.

Maybe you wake up, do what needs to be done, come home, scroll, sleep… and do it all again.

Nothing is terribly wrong. And yet, something doesn’t feel right.

Maybe something in you is asking for more.

THE FEELING BEHIND ALIVE

You stopped asking yourself — “Am I actually happy?”

You miss a version of yourself you can’t quite explain. The one who laughed without thinking. Who got excited about little things. Who wanted to explore. Who had ideas, dreams, curiosity.

Maybe you don’t need a completely new life. Maybe you need to find yourself inside the life you already have.

I created ALIVE because I believe life is meant to be more than simply getting through it.

I want to help people pause, reconnect with their energy, discover what they’re naturally good at, explore what genuinely matters to them, and create a life that feels like their own.
