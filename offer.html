A 2-Day ALIVE Experience

ALIVE

From surviving your routine to reconnecting with your life.

Sometimes nothing is obviously wrong.

You are working. You are functioning. You are doing what you are supposed to do.

And yet, somewhere along the way, life starts feeling repetitive, heavy or disconnected.

This experience is an invitation to pause and ask: What do I actually want my life to feel like?

Day 01

Remember You

Before life got serious.

Day one is about reconnecting with the person underneath the expectations, routines and roles you have collected.

  • Who was I before life got serious?
  • What have I stopped doing that once made me feel alive?
  • What expectations am I carrying?
  • What has been quietly draining my energy?
  • What have I been avoiding?
Day 02

Design Your Life

Make it feel like yours.

Day two moves from awareness into possibility, intention and real action.

  • What do I actually want more of?
  • What do I want less of?
  • What would a life that feels like mine look like?
  • What have I been postponing?
  • What can I start doing now?

This isn't just a talk.

You won't spend two days sitting and listening. The ALIVE Experience is designed to make you experience the ideas.

Is this for you?

Maybe you are not looking for a complete life makeover. Maybe you simply know that something inside you wants more.

  • You feel disconnected from yourself.
  • You are functioning, but not feeling fulfilled.
  • You feel drained, stuck or low in energy.
  • You miss an older, more playful version of yourself.
  • You feel burdened by expectations or social image.
  • You want more clarity about what you really want.
  • You are ready to stop postponing your life.

Come back to yourself.

The intention isn't to leave you with a hundred things to change.

It's to leave you with greater awareness, renewed energy, a clearer sense of what matters to you — and a real first step you are ready to take.
